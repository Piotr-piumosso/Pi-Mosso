About the duo

Music that does more than accompany a moment. It shapes the atmosphere around it.

We combine classical craft with a contemporary sense of form. Our performances are elegant, expressive and built with the acoustic space, occasion and emotional arc in mind.

Più Mosso is built around musical quality and artistic clarity.

We focus on sound culture, dramaturgy and a refined stage presence. This allows our performances to work naturally in weddings, representative ceremonies, recitals and concert programs alike.

Zofia Andersz • violin

Sound shaped with lightness, nuance and natural stage freedom.

A violinist currently continuing her studies under Prof. Dorota Kwiecińska. She performs in chamber festivals, solo concerts and orchestral projects spanning classical, film and popular repertoire. Her playing brings lyricism, precision and a strong sense of storytelling.

Piotr Górski • organ and piano

Interpretation grounded in virtuosity, dramaturgy and concert experience.

An award-winning organist and pianist, graduate of the Poznań Academy of Music with honours. He performs regularly, teaches, and develops projects where classical tradition meets a modern sense of form, pacing and architectural space.
