About

Zofia Andersz violin

A violinist whose bow paints stories in light and shadow. She holds a bachelor’s degree in the violin class of Prof. Dorota Kwiecińska and is currently pursuing her master’s studies with the same professor. She has appeared at chamber festivals and as a soloist, blending classical tradition with a contemporary sense of colour. Zofia has taken part in a wide range of orchestral projects — from classical and film music to popular repertoire — and is also active on the pedagogical side of the stand.

Piotr Górski organ & piano

A concert organist and pianist, regularly invited to perform on Polish stages and at festivals. Prizewinner of organ competitions: First Prize – Giżycko 2022, Distinction – Kraków 2023, Third Prize – Rumia 2024, First Prize – Castellana Grotte 2025. Graduate of the Academy of Music in Poznań (with honours). He completed his master’s studies in the class of Prof. Dr. habil. Sławomir Kamiński and Dr. Jakub Pankowiak, and his bachelor’s studies in the class of Prof. Dr. habil. Elżbieta Karolak and Dr. Jarosław Tarnawski. He has also taken part in masterclasses with organists such as Wolfgang Zerer, Wolfgang Seifen, Sylvain Pluyaut, Karol Mossakowski, Tomasz Orlow, and Krzysztof Lukas. His interpretations combine virtuosity with a strong sense of dramatic expression. He serves as organist of the Holy Trinity Church in Nowe Miasto nad Wartą and devotes part of his activity to teaching.
