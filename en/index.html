About Us

Zofia Andersz violin

A violinist whose bow paints stories filled with light and shadow. She has performed at chamber festivals and solo recitals, blending classical core repertoire with a contemporary sensitivity. She also shares her craft through educational work.

Piotr Górski organ  and piano

A concert organist and pianist regularly invited to Polish festivals and stages. His interpretations unite virtuosity with vivid dramaturgy. He serves as organist of Holy Trinity Church in Nowe Miasto nad Wartą and devotes part of his energy to teaching.
