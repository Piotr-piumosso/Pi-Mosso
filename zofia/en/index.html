About the artist

Violinist. Student at the Poznań Academy of Music.

Zofia Andersz studies at the Academy of Music in Poznań under prof. Dorota Kwiecińska. She focuses on chamber music and solo violin recitals. She performs in churches, concert halls and cultural venues.

Day to day she plays in orchestral and chamber projects, alongside her studies at the Academy of Music in Poznań. Solo, she performs at ceremonies and recitals for cultural institutions.

The programme is settled after a conversation about the venue and character of the event - there's no fixed price list or menu of pieces to pick from.

How it works

Choosing the programme step by step

  • agreeing on the type of event, venue and format,
  • a repertoire proposal or direction for the programme,
  • matching the length and character of the music to the event,
  • final confirmation of details in a clear process.

It's fine to start without a programme in mind too - just describe the venue and mood of the event, and she'll take care of the rest.
