About the artist

Competition prizewinner. Graduate with distinction.

Piotr Górski is a versatile musician - organist and pianist - a graduate of the Academy of Music in Poznań with a diploma with distinction. He performs in concert halls and churches in Poland and abroad. He looks for projects where classical music gets a chance to sound in an unusual venue or an unconventional programme.

  • 2024 3rd prize - Rumia Organ Competition
  • 2023 Honourable mention - 9th National Bronisław Rutkowski Organ Competition, Kraków
  • 2022 1st prize - Giżycko Organ Competition

Offer

Three most common areas of collaboration.

The scope can be matched to the venue, the instrument, the character of the event and the expected length of the programme.

Ceremonies

Musical setting for ceremonies

  • church and civil ceremonies,
  • help choosing pieces and the order of moments,
  • calm execution without organisational chaos.
Concerts

Recitals and artistic events

  • churches, concert halls, cultural centres and festivals,
  • organ or piano formats,
  • programme matched to the character of the event.
Teaching

Individual lessons and tutoring

Learning to play, exam preparation and improvisation - all in one place, with one teacher.

  • piano, organ, accordion, saxophone,
  • tutoring in music-school subjects (ear training, harmony, music history, theory and more),
  • music for students in general schools,
  • improvisation lessons (classical, jazz, liturgical).

Music school

Lessons, tutoring, improvisation.

Individual classes for students at every stage - from first steps at the instrument to diploma exam preparation.

Instruments

Piano · Organ · Accordion · Saxophone

Learning to play from scratch or refining technique. Programme matched to the student's goals - recital, exam, personal enjoyment.

  • technique, interpretation, sight-reading,
  • classical, contemporary and popular repertoire,

Music-school tutoring

All music school subjects

Help catching up on gaps and preparing for promotional and diploma exams.

  • ear training and dictation,
  • rules of music and theory,
  • harmony and musical analysis,
  • music history (including extended-level exams),
  • music appreciation, rhythmics, accompaniment.
General schools

Music in regular school

Music tutoring for primary and secondary school students - tests, quizzes, final exams.

  • music, primary school grades 4-7,
  • music history - extended-level exams,
  • preparation for music competitions.
Improvisation

Learning improvisation

A skill not taught in music school, which opens up the instrument in a whole new way.

  • classical and liturgical improvisation on organ,
  • jazz improvisation on piano,
  • harmonising melodies and playing by ear,
  • basso continuo and historical styles.
Repertoire

Repertoire tailored, not off-the-shelf.

There's no single list I send to everyone. Every programme is built around the specific venue, instrument and character of the occasion.

Classical and sacred music

Bach, Buxtehude, Franck, Vierne. And anything that works well on the specific organ or piano at hand.

Organ or piano recital

A solo programme with a dramaturgy, not a random set. A considered whole from the first note to the last.

Film themes and requests

If you have a specific piece in mind, tell me. If it can be played well, I'll play it.

How it works

Choosing the programme step by step

  • agreeing on the type of event, venue and available instrument,
  • a repertoire proposal or direction for the programme,
  • matching the length and character of the music to the event,
  • final confirmation of details in a clear process.

If you don't have any preferences yet, a description of the venue and mood is enough. I'll take care of the rest.
